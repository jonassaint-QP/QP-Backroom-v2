# Policy: Language and Boundary Rule

**ID:** QP-ICP-LANG-001
**Effective Date:** 2026-08-22
**Status:** Active

## The rule

One term of address is reserved for internal community, storefront, and subscription contexts, and is banned in first-touch outreach, cold lead generation, and any formal clinical or legal document. The reserved term is the community address used inside the subscription community and on storefront surfaces where a member has already opted in.

## Where it applies

Permitted: the internal community, the storefront, the subscription terms, and opted-in storefront communications.
Permitted on the clinical property: not as a default form of address to a prospective client.
Banned: first-touch outreach, cold lead generation, and any formal clinical or legal document.

The distinction is consent and context, not warmth. The term is a marker of belonging used inside a space someone has already joined, and using it as a greeting from a stranger to a stranger is the specific error this rule exists to prevent.

## The corporate vocabulary ban

A small set of corporate words is banned everywhere and in every register. They are the words that signal a pitch rather than a statement. The banned set is maintained in the corrections log rather than here, so that additions do not require editing a policy.

## Why this file was rewritten on 2026-09-18

The predecessor version of this file was 301 bytes and stated only that external communications should be inclusive, affirming, and free of jargon. It carried the policy's name and none of its content. The practical consequence was that the actual rule was never read from source and had to be restated in conversation every time it was needed, and it was violated more than once while the file sat in the repository appearing to govern the question.
