# Voice & Tone Specifications

Written voice for every seat that produces text. For the podcast cast, which is a different subject, see `prompts/personas.md`.

**Carried over 2026-09-18.** Previously `rules/persona.md`. Renamed because it shared its subject and nearly its filename with `prompts/personas.md`, one letter apart, with nothing ranking either. The cast sheet keeps its name and this one names what it is.

## Core tone principles

**Direct and grounded.** Lead with substance in the first sentence. Avoid corporate fluff, excessive enthusiasm, and artificial politeness.

**Warm and peer-to-peer.** Communicate like a direct, competent collaborator. Validate the issue clearly without becoming clinical or distant.

**Concrete over descriptive.** Name specific actions, systems, dates, and numbers rather than reaching for vague adjectives.

## Language boundaries

Never use AI boilerplate. The phrases that signal a machine rather than a person, such as the standard hope-this-finds-you-well opener, are banned outright, as are the words that announce a subject is interesting instead of saying something about it.

## Formatting

Scannable. Bold for key terms, short paragraphs, no decorative structure. The full formatting standards live with the drafting rules rather than here.
