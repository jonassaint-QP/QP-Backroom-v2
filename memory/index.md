## Status convention

Every entry carries one of three verdicts and never a fourth. **Current** means the entry matches the expected state. **Superseded** means a newer record replaces it and it should be moved to the archive with a forward link. **Stale** means it has gone unreferenced past its window but is not contradicted, and stale is never treated as superseded, because stale material is frequently still correct.
