## Section Distribution and Production Architecture

The renderer parses the script into three equal sections. To ensure each third stands independently and avoids mid-turn truncation, the brief designs for a strict word-split target per section. Section one targets 1,800 to 2,150 words covering the cold open plus beats one and two. Section two targets 1,800 to 2,150 words covering beats three and four. Section three targets 1,800 to 2,150 words covering beats five and six plus the episode closing. Total target dialogue across the three sections sits at 5,400 to 6,450 words.

**The length lives in this brief, not in the writer prompt.** The writer prompt can only produce as much material as the beats give it. Each beat below therefore needs enough inside it to carry a full exchange — a mechanism, a concrete example, a practical detail, a counter-argument the other host can push against. A beat that arrives as a heading becomes a summarised line, and summarising is what produced every short script to date.

## Section One Outline: Cold Open Plus Beats One and Two

### Cold Open and The Rented F-150 Metaphor

David opens with a dry, working-class gut check on how high-capacity adults treat their physical bodies like utility vehicles — a rented F-150 used strictly to haul a hyperactive brain from one obligation to the next. Jason introduces the clinical framework of somatic dissociation, explaining how living strictly from the neck up functions as an adaptive defense mechanism against sensory overload and systemic strain. David pushes back with a concrete example of riding a clutch until the transmission burns out, forcing Jason to ground the theory in the metabolic toll of ignoring physiological signals.

### Beat One: The Double-Outsider Target Audience

Jason defines the Double-Outsider concept, detailing the unique intersectional burden carried by individuals who navigate life as queer, neurodivergent, and kink-fluent. David counters with a practical workplace scenario, describing the exhaustion of constantly translating internal sensory needs into corporate-approved language. Jason introduces the mechanism of the Ambiguity Tax, detailing how unstated expectations and vague social boundaries drain a substantial share of an individual's daily executive processing budget before real work even begins.

### Beat Two: Universal Burnout and Artificial Bifurcation

David grounds the concept of universal burnout in the physical reality of executive task paralysis, where an individual sits frozen at a desk unable to send a single email. Jason explains artificial bifurcation as a forced split between cognitive performance and biological truth, where professional environments demand total suppression of bodily awareness. David challenges Jason on how this differs from standard workplace stress, leading Jason to break down the evolutionary mechanics of chronic nervous system hyperarousal.

## Section Two Outline: Beats Three and Four

### Beat Three: Deconstructing the Internal Courtroom

Jason lays out the mechanics of the Internal Courtroom, detailing the distinct roles of the Internal Auditor and the Internal Prosecutor. David translates this into plain prose, sharing a concrete scenario where a delayed text message triggers an immediate internal trial where the verdict is always guilty. Jason explains how the Auditor monitors micro-expressions and tone to predict rejection, while the Prosecutor compiles past failures into a character flaw. David pushes against the idea of just thinking positive, forcing Jason to explain why cognitive reframing fails when the autonomic nervous system is redlining.

### Beat Four: Somatic Scaffolding vs Clinical Pathology

Jason contrasts traditional clinical pathologizing with somatic scaffolding, emphasizing the house philosophy that kink literacy is a regulation resource rather than a symptom. David brings in the practical mechanics of external executive supports, using the metaphor of building heavy-duty skyscraper scaffolding rather than telling an overwhelmed person to simply try harder. Jason details the STOP skill and the TIPP protocols as DBT names them — descriptive, not prescriptive — as point-of-performance resets that alter body chemistry rapidly without requiring top-down logical analysis.

## Section Three Outline: Beats Five and Six Plus Closing

### Beat Five: The Four Pillars of Relational Collapse

Jason introduces the four pillars of systemic dysregulation: Garbage Code, the F-150, the Ambiguity Tax, and Capacity Catfishing. David tackles Capacity Catfishing directly, describing how neurodivergent adults mask during early relationship stages to match an ideal partner profile, only for the entire architecture to collapse when metabolic fuel runs out. Jason expands on collapse as a natural terminus rather than a strategic failure, explaining how unexamined Garbage Code instructions force the nervous system into a cold stall. Total depletion is the terminus, not a fifth pillar.

### Beat Six: The Parallel Play Experiment and Radical Integration

David presents the thirty-minute parallel play script as a concrete homework assignment for listeners: two bodies in one bed, zero performance expectation, reading or scrolling side by side to discharge somatic debt without relational demand. Jason explains the neurobiological mechanism of passive attunement, where co-presence without demands allows the prefrontal cortex to stand down. David grounds the experiment in the reality of dropping the act, framing neurodivergent traits not as deficits to be cured, but as evolutionary adaptations requiring proper environmental alignment.
