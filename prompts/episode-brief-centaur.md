## SECTION 1 — COLD OPEN + BEATS 1–2

Jason reframes: the truck is not the problem; the idea that you own a truck and not a body is the problem. Introduce the Double Outsider audience: queer, neurodivergent, kink-fluent, built for systems that never designed a seat for them. Name the artificial bifurcation — the neck-up existence corporate life demands, the biological needs suppressed underneath.

Beat 1 — The Centaur Metaphor, Anatomy of the Self: Human torso = executive functioning, logic, strategic thinking, the social masking required to navigate a cis-heteronormative, neurotypical society. Horse body = animal truth: raw somatic instincts, autonomic signals, biological drives, kinesthetic processing.

Beat 2 — Mismatched Rhythms: society pathologizes the hybridity. Human logic and horse instincts do not move in a linear 9-to-5 cubicle structure. David's mechanic frame: you don't blame a horse for not idling like a sedan.

## SECTION 2 — BEATS 3–4
Beat 3 — The Internal Conflict & Biological Costs: The Internal Auditor — the hypervigilant surveillance mechanism enforcing neurotypical standards and demanding impossible consistency. Prefrontal cortex "brownout": litigating and shaming the body burns glucose, drains executive fuel, ends in metabolic bankruptcy and burnout. Why cognitive solutions fail: you cannot think your way out of a depleted prefrontal cortex; more thinking is not the tool for a thinking-part failure. Jason flags the clinical parallel: this is the somatic story of minority stress — chronic vigilance taxed at the level of the nervous system, not just the mind.

Beat 4 — Somatic Interventions & Regulation Tools: Commanding the Perimeter — building an internal safe harbor where strategy and instinct align.
