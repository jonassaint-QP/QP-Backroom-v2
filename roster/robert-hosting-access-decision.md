# Decision Record — Robert's Hosting Access

**Decision: hosting access is GRANTED.** Recorded 2026-10-02 by the founder, in response to the binary
carried in the seat interface review.

---

## What the binary was

The estate's rule is that for any seat, the choice between **granting hosting access** and **recording
the deploy as founder-held in writing** must be documented explicitly. There is no third option.

Robert's seat had carried neither. Its operating spec declares four walls — only the clinical sandbox
repository is mounted, the storefront repository and the blog host and the podcast workspace are not
reachable, the dependency tree is read-only, and there is no push path — **and not one of them mentions
hosting access, which is the exact wall that makes the deploy impossible.**

The underlying condition, stated in the roster and in the handover skeleton: a vendor purge was merged
to clinical main at 23:51Z on 18 September, and not one public page has changed, because the deploy
requires hosting access the seat does not hold.

**That condition is now resolved in the affirmative. Access is granted.**

---

## What this changes

**The seat can deploy.** The blocker was never the repository or the build — it was the right to publish.
With hosting access granted, the merged vendor purge has a path to production for the first time since
18 September.

**The seat's own spec is now incorrect and must be updated.** It declares four walls and omits the one
that governed the seat's central failure. A successor reading it would not know the deploy wall existed,
or that it has been lifted.

**The stalled chain is unblocked.** A merged change with no deploy path is the clearest form of a stalled
item. It is no longer one.

---

## What remains, and who owns it

**The access must be provisioned at the hosting provider.** A decision recorded in the repository does
not create a credential or a seat in a third-party console. **The founder provisions it; the seat cannot
provision its own access and no AI seat holds administrative rights on any provider surface.**

**The seat then verifies rather than assumes.** By its own standing rule, it verifies the live surface
rather than the source. **The test is a public page that has actually changed** — not a successful build,
not a green check, not a granted permission.

**And the record stays binary.** If provisioning does not complete before the outgoing team's deadline,
the entry supersedes to the founder-held form rather than being left open. **A decision carried as
pending is the same defect this record exists to close.**

---

## The three open items this bears on

**One.** The 1 October repository go-live date has fired with no outcome recorded in either Robert's or
Adam's folder. **A decision record for it is cited as a source elsewhere in the estate.** With access
granted, the outcome should now be recordable rather than outstanding.

**Two.** The merge-lane sentence in Robert's operating spec — that the estate holds zero open pull
requests and nothing was ever submitted to a review gate — is now materially false. Three pull requests
were opened and merged on 2 October.

**Three.** The homepage claim about a suspended jurisdiction's credentials, and the retired-jurisdiction
sitemap route, are both now deployable fixes rather than blocked ones. **A defect that can be fixed and
has not been is a different kind of finding than one that cannot be fixed at all.**
