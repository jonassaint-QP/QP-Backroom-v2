## What the stream is

**Coaching is a non-clinical service, delivered clinically by the founder, priced at $150 for one hour
and $200 for two hours.** Two live payment links were created on 2 October and are taking money for the
first time in the practice's history.

---

## Ownership — two halves, one offer

**Jason owns the operation.** Intake logistics, scheduling, delivery and the operational record of a
coaching engagement. **This is the half that decides whether the service can be delivered as sold.**

**Michael owns the sale.** Client and customer acquisition, the offer, and any discounting or promotional
structure used to bring people in. **This half is DORMANT until 2026-10-10 — see the amendment above.**

**They are deliberately separate.** The seat that promises and the seat that delivers are not the same
seat, and neither can rewrite the other's half.

---

## Financially — a time-boxed exception, not a lane

**Coaching money settles wherever the Stripe account settles, and this is recorded as an EXCEPTION with
an end date of 2026-10-10.**

**Stated plainly, because a successor must not read this as a settled arrangement:**

**Coaching is a third revenue stream with no lane assignment.** It is not Lane 1 and it is not Lane 2.
**QP-POL-002 is unchanged and remains in force.** Stripe is prohibited on both lanes and this exception
does not alter that.

**The exposure this carries:** Stripe is the processor that had already been moving clinical money via a
cancelled intake platform, and the firewall exists to end exactly that arrangement. **Money from clinical
work moving through a commercial processor, outside both lanes and outside the financial seat's sight, is
the condition the two-lane architecture was built to remove.**

**The exception exists because the links are live and the decision is the founder's.** It closes on
10 October, when the lane assignment is revisited with the handover complete.

## What the financial seat needs in order to watch it

**Mark cannot monitor a stream his folder does not name.** The following must be carried in his folder
for the loop to close:

**One.** Owned by Jason (operation) and Michael (sale), **with the sale half dormant until 2026-10-10.**

**Two.** Settles to the Stripe account, on an exception expiring 2026-10-10.

**Three.** Two published price points — $150 for one hour, $200 for two hours — and any deviation
requires a written reason.

**Four.** A signal class for the stream, so a payment that arrives is visible as a coaching payment and
not as unclassified commercial income.

## The rule interaction, held for when the dormancy lifts

**This is retained on the record but is not currently engaged, since no discounting is authorised before
10 October.**

**A discount is a price change.** Under the compliance ruling adopted on 2 October, any change to a
claim, credential, price, jurisdiction or legal endpoint voids the clearance and re-checks it. **So every
promotional offer resets compliance on that offer.**

**And the financial seat carries a pricing rule** requiring a written reason for any deviation from
published rate.

**Consequence:** when Michael's authority goes live, a defined floor and discretion limit should exist,
or every deviation is a founder-level decision twice over.
